Before you go

A thoughtful trip checklist

  1. Check entry requirements. Confirm current visa and passport guidance with the relevant Nigerian authorities.
  2. Ask about health preparation. Consult a qualified travel-health professional well ahead of departure.
  3. Plan how you will get around. Research local transport, realistic travel times, and trusted local contacts.
  4. Check current advice. Review recent official travel advisories for each place you hope to visit.
  5. Leave room for local knowledge. Opening times and access can change; confirm details with hosts before traveling.

Naija Atlas is an educational project, not an official travel, medical, or booking service. Verify important decisions with current authoritative sources.
