N Naija Atlas

WDD 131 · Website plan

Project blueprint

A guide to finding your way through Nigeria.

Naija Atlas will help curious visitors discover the places, people, and practical details that make a trip memorable.

01 / Identity

Site name

N Naija Atlas

Naija Atlas pairs “Naija,” a familiar name for Nigeria, with “Atlas,” a book of maps. Together, the name promises a friendly guide to exploring the country's regions, culture, and travel experiences.

02 / Purpose

Site purpose

Naija Atlas is a practical, welcoming starting point for people planning a visit or learning about Nigeria. It will bring destination highlights, cultural context, local food, and useful travel-planning information together in one responsive guide.

  • Explore: an overview of Nigeria and its regions.
  • Discover: destination and culture profiles with photos and visitor highlights.
  • Plan: a short trip-interest form and a saved list of places to explore.

03 / Audience

Visitor scenarios

Likely visitors include first-time travelers, members of the Nigerian diaspora, and anyone looking for a useful introduction to the country's places and culture.

“Which destinations can I visit to experience Nigeria's landscapes and city life?”
“What Nigerian foods and cultural experiences should I look for during my trip?”
“How can I save places I am interested in and send a question about planning a visit?”

04 / Visual system

Color schema

The palette draws on Nigeria's green landscape and warm market colors while keeping long-form text crisp and readable.

Forest green #175B43 Brand, headings, and primary actions
Market gold #E5AE3A Small highlights, borders, and active details
Warm paper #F7F5EF Page background and quiet content areas
Charcoal #202923 Body copy and high-contrast text

05 / Voice

Typography

Headings · Playfair Display

Places with a story.

Body and controls · Open Sans

Clear, comfortable text for destination details, travel notes, and forms.

06 / Structure

Home page wireframes

Both layouts keep the brand and navigation first, then lead with a destination image and a clear route into useful content.
